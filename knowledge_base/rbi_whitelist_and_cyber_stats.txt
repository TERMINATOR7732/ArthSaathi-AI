=== RED FLAG INDICATORS: ILLEGAL LOAN APPS ===

WARNING SIGNS (Do NOT use these types of apps):
- No RBI registration number visible in app/website
- Requires full repayment in 7–14 days
- APR exceeds 36% (often 200%–500%)
- Demands access to contacts and photo gallery
- No physical address or grievance officer listed
- Disburses loan without video KYC or credit check
- Threatens harassment calls if payment delayed
- Uses WhatsApp/Telegram for "customer support" only

=== CYBERCRIME STATISTICS INDIA 2023-24 ===

Financial Cybercrime Scale:
- Total cyber fraud complaints (2023): 11.28 lakh (MHA NCRB)
- Total financial loss reported: ₹7,488.63 crore (CY 2023)
- UPI fraud incidents: 95,000+ cases (NPCI data, FY2023-24)
- Digital lending app complaints: 2,562 FIRs registered (RBI data FY2023)

Top Fraud Vectors:
1. Fake investment/trading apps: 47.25% of losses
2. Illegal loan apps (recovery harassment): 17.3%
3. OTP/SIM swap fraud: 12.8%
4. Fake KYC update scams: 9.4%
5. QR code "receive money" scams: 6.1%
6. Screen-sharing (remote access) scams: 4.2%
7. Other: 2.95%

State-wise High Fraud Zones (2023):
- Maharashtra: Highest UPI fraud cases
- Rajasthan (Mewat): Highest cyber crime per capita
- Karnataka (Bengaluru): Highest tech-enabled fraud
- Haryana: High SIM swap fraud

Recovery Statistics:
- Amount frozen within golden hour: ₹1,127 crore (FY2023-24)
- Amount recovered/refunded: ₹153 crore (FY2023-24)
- Cases resolved via 1930 helpline: 7.6 lakh (FY2023-24)
