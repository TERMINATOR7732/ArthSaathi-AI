=== SECTION 1: UPI FRAUD PREVENTION ===

1. UPI PIN is NEVER shared with anyone — not even with your bank, NPCI, or payment app support.
2. A UPI 'Collect Request' means MONEY LEAVES your account. Always verify before approving.
3. Scammers impersonate NPCI/banks via fake "KYC Update" SMS or WhatsApp messages.
4. Screen sharing apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer) are common fraud tools — never install them for banking.
5. QR Codes are for PAYMENTS only. Scanning a QR to "receive" money is always a scam.
6. Your UPI ID does not need "activation" — activation fee requests are scams.
7. OTPs are one-time; once you share one, your account can be immediately compromised.
8. Report UPI fraud within 3 days to trigger RBI Zero-Liability Rule (Circular DBR.No.Leg.BC.78/09.07.005/2017-18).
9. Always use official apps: PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM — never third-party clones.
10. Verify beneficiary name shown on screen before confirming any UPI transaction.

=== SECTION 2: PREDATORY LOAN APP IDENTIFICATION ===

1. Legal NBFC/bank loan apps must be registered with RBI. Check: https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/English/scripts/NBFCs.aspx
2. APR (Annual Percentage Rate) above 36% is a strong indicator of predatory lending.
3. PM Mudra Yojana offers loans at 8.5%–14% APR — benchmark for fair lending.
4. Predatory apps demand full repayment in 7–14 days (not EMIs), maximizing effective APR to 200–500%.
5. Illegal apps access your contacts, gallery, and location to harass defaulters.
6. Processing fees disguised as "insurance" or "security deposit" are red flags.
7. Legitimate lenders never disburse loans without proper KYC and credit assessment.
8. RBI Circular DOR.FIN.HC.No.37/20.06.018/2022-23 bans unauthorized digital lending apps.
9. Always demand a signed loan agreement with total cost of credit disclosed before disbursement.
10. If contacted by an illegal app, report to Cybercrime Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in

=== SECTION 3: EMERGENCY CYBERCRIME RESPONSE ===

1. National Cybercrime Helpline: 1930 (24×7 active)
2. Cybercrime Reporting Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in
3. RBI Zero-Liability Rule: Report within 3 working days for full protection (Circular DBR.No.Leg.BC.78).
4. After 3 days but within 7 days: up to 25,000 liability may apply depending on the bank.
5. "Golden Hour" Rule: Block card/freeze UPI immediately via bank app or 1930 hotline.
6. NPCI Dispute Resolution: Raise a dispute via your UPI app within 24 hours for faster resolution.
7. Gather evidence: screenshots, transaction IDs, chat logs before reporting.
8. File an FIR at nearest Cyber Cell or local police station after reporting on 1930.
9. Inform your bank's fraud desk directly via the official number on the back of your debit card.
10. Do not delete any messages or transaction history — these are evidence.

=== SECTION 4: PM JAN DHAN YOJANA & GOVERNMENT SCHEMES ===

1. PMJDY accounts have zero minimum balance and come with a free RuPay debit card.
2. PMJDY overdraft facility: up to ₹10,000 available after 6 months of active usage.
3. PM Kisan Samman Nidhi: ₹6,000/year directly to farmers' accounts in 3 instalments.
4. PMSBY (Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana): ₹2 lakh accidental cover at just ₹20/year.
5. PMJJBY (Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana): ₹2 lakh life cover at ₹436/year.
6. Atal Pension Yojana: Guaranteed pension of ₹1,000–₹5,000/month after age 60.
7. Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana: 8.2% interest (Q1 FY2025) for girl child savings.
8. NSC (National Savings Certificate): 7.7% guaranteed return, 5-year lock-in.
9. PMMY (Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana): Loans up to ₹10 lakh for micro enterprises.
   - Shishu: up to ₹50,000 | Kishore: ₹50,001–₹5 lakh | Tarun: ₹5 lakh–₹10 lakh

=== SECTION 5: DIGITAL PAYMENT SAFETY BEST PRACTICES ===

1. Enable two-factor authentication (UPI PIN + biometric) on all payment apps.
2. Set UPI transaction limits to your typical usage to contain losses from fraud.
3. Use VPA (Virtual Payment Address) instead of sharing bank account numbers.
4. Regularly check linked accounts in your UPI app and remove unused ones.
5. Never save UPI PIN in notes apps, cloud storage, or share via chat.
6. Install payment apps only from official Play Store / App Store.
7. Update payment apps regularly to get latest security patches.
8. Be suspicious of "cashback" or "lottery" requests that ask you to enter your PIN first.
9. RBI mandates all banks to provide a 24×7 helpline for blocking compromised accounts.
10. Use BHIM app (by NPCI) as the baseline secure UPI application — government-backed.
