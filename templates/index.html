NPCI & RBI Rules

3 Golden Rules of UPI

  • 1
    PIN is only for sending: You never enter your UPI PIN to receive money or cashback.
  • 2
    QR codes pay, never credit: Scanning any QR code always debits funds from your account.
  • 3
    3-day zero liability: Report unauthorized transactions within 3 working days (RBI Circular DBR.No.Leg.BC.78).

Calculate the simplified annualized borrowing cost for any short-term loan. This is an estimate only — not a legally defined APR. Use it to compare offers and identify predatory rates.

How is this calculated?

cost (%) = ((repayment − principal) / principal) × (365 / days) × 100

Example: ₹10,000 loan, ₹12,500 repayment, 15-day tenure → 608.3% annualized cost.

The RBI considers rates above 36% a strong indicator of predatory lending for regulated NBFCs. Always check whether a lender is RBI-registered at sachet.rbi.org.in.

This formula is a simplified illustrative estimate. Actual loan cost disclosures are governed by RBI Fair Practices Code (DOR.STR.REC.68/21.04.048/2023-24). Always request a written loan agreement before borrowing.

Immediate Fraud Response

If you have been defrauded or notice unauthorized transactions, follow these steps immediately. Time is critical — fund freezing is most effective within the first hour.

  1. 1
    Call 1930 immediately

    National Cybercrime Helpline — 24×7, toll-free. Triggers automated fund-freeze across 400+ banks via the CFCFRMS network. Do this first, before anything else.

  2. 2
    File a complaint online

    Visit cybercrime.gov.in. Submit transaction IDs, debit SMS screenshots, and any chat logs as evidence.

  3. 3
    Block your card and UPI immediately

    Use your bank's app, IVR helpline, or call the number printed on the back of your debit card.

  4. 4
    Notify your bank in writing within 3 working days

    RBI Circular DBR.No.Leg.BC.78/09.07.005/2017-18 provides consumer protection for unauthorized transactions reported promptly. Contact your bank for the exact procedure — outcomes depend on individual circumstances and bank policy.

  5. 5
    Preserve all evidence

    Do not delete any SMS, call logs, transaction records, or chat screenshots. These are required for investigation.
